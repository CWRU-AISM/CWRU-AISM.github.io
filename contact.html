Joining the lab

We seek students with mechanical, electrical, or computer engineering (or computer science) backgrounds who are interested in applying machine learning to manufacturing and robotics.

PhD students should apply through the CWRU Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, and email Prof. Wang a CV and brief note indicating which of our projects you are interested in. CWRU undergrads and masters students may also inquire about research positions by email.
