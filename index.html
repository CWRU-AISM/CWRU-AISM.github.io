Augmented Intelligence for Smart Manufacturing

The Augmented Intelligence for Smart Manufacturing (AISM) Lab is a research group in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Case Western Reserve University (CWRU) in Cleveland, Ohio, led by Prof. Peng (Edward) Wang.

We engineer machine learning that works on the factory floor. This involves combining data-driven models with domain knowledge to make them robust to changes in machines, materials, and operating conditions, while also keeping them inexpensive enough for small manufacturers to adopt.

Application areas include predictive maintenance, robot learning, welding quality prediction, in-process sensing/imaging. Partners include General Motors, NVIDIA, Miller Electric, University of Kentucky, NSF HAMMER Engineering Research Center.
